Process

How a project runs

01

Scope in writing

You describe the outcome. We write down what is included, what is not, and what we need from you before anything starts.

02

Fixed price, short cycle

One number and a delivery window agreed up front. Most work lands in days, not quarters. You watch it on a staging URL.

03

Handover you can keep

Source, credentials, run instructions and a plain-language note on how to change things later. You are not locked to us.

No account manager layer and no offshore handoff: the person who scoped the work is the person who builds it.
