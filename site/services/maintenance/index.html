Care plan & maintenance

A care plan for the site you already have

Most sites are not broken. They drift: a feed changes shape, a certificate expires, a form stops delivering without an error. A plan turns that into a known monthly number instead of an emergency.

Care plan. A fixed monthly for keeping a live site from breaking without anyone noticing. The $90 tier watches uptime, scheduled runs, error logs, form deliveries and what the AI crawlers actually fetch, and sends a one-page report each month. The $350 tier adds four hours of changes, fixes caused by third-party breakage, and a monthly re-run of your buyers' questions against the answer engines. What does the plan watch?

The failures worth paying to hear about early are the quiet ones: a run that reports success while writing nothing, a checkout that returns 200 and saves no order, an email that leaves the form and never arrives.

Uptime and pages

The pages that carry the business, checked from outside. A page that answers with an error, or with the right status and the wrong content, is treated as down.

Scheduled work

Every run we built, with its exit status, duration and last successful time. Silence is a failure state, not a pass.

Forms and orders

A test submission once a day, so a broken mail path is found by us rather than by a customer who assumed they had been ignored.

The slow decay

Certificate expiry, dependency updates that change behavior, feeds that changed shape, search coverage that dropped after someone else's deploy.

Answer engines

Which AI crawlers actually fetch your pages, how many pages they take, and whether an assistant still describes you the same way it did last month. The crawl numbers come from the logs, not from a tool's estimate.

What the plan does not watch

No ranking guarantees and no promise about what an assistant will say about you. We can show what was crawled, what was retrieved and who was named when we asked; nobody controls the answer itself.
