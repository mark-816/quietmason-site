Services

Four kinds of work, in the order we do most of it. Every engagement is fixed-scope and ends with a written handover.

Most requested

Automation & data pipelines

We build the boring machinery that a business outgrows doing by hand: collecting data from websites and APIs, cleaning it into one shape, storing it somewhere sensible, and running the whole thing on a schedule with alerts when it breaks. Typical builds include price and catalog collectors, order and notification automations, lead lists enriched from public sources, and reporting pipelines that write themselves. The parts that fail in practice (rate limits, layout changes, partial runs, silent errors) are the parts we design for, because we run pipelines of our own every day. You get the source, the run instructions and a documented way to change the schedule without us.

Typical scopes: a single workflow $250–1,000 · multi-step integrations $1,000–3,000 · scraping and enrichment pipelines $700–4,600

Search foundation

Technical SEO

This is the work that decides whether anything else you publish can rank: what gets crawled and indexed, whether the page can be rendered, whether the markup means anything, whether the internal links point at the pages you care about. We start from Search Console rather than from opinion (coverage, queries, clicks, per page), and we fix the things whose effect we can re-measure afterwards. Deliverables are a findings list with a priority order, the fixes applied, and a before/after read you can verify yourself in your own account. We do not sell backlinks, and we say so on the pricing page, because that is the part of this industry that produces nothing.

Typical scopes: audits from $400 · remediation scoped per finding · optional monthly measurement

See technical SEO work
Early, but real

AI search visibility

Assistants and AI answer boxes increasingly sit in front of the click, and they quote sources that are unambiguous about who they are and what they claim. We work on that surface: one consistent entity across the site and its profiles, structured data that matches what is actually on the page, pages written so a machine can lift a correct sentence without inventing one. What we will not do is promise that a model will recommend you. Nobody can, and anyone who offers that is guessing. We measure what is measurable: structured-data coverage, entity consistency, and which of your pages are being retrieved and cited.

Typical scope: bundled with technical SEO work, priced per site

Websites & fixes

Building it, moving it, or making it fast. Stores and landing pages for products that need to sell in English; migrations between hosts and platforms that keep the URLs and the rankings intact; performance work on pages that are technically fine but slow enough to lose people. We build plain HTML and PHP by default, because it stays fast, stays cheap to host, and does not depend on a subscription to keep working. Send a URL and we will tell you what we would change before we quote.

Typical scopes: landing pages from $300 · migrations and speed work from $500
