Four kinds of website job

Build

A store or landing page for a product that needs to sell abroad: pages, product data, checkout flow, shipping and returns wording, tracking and the structured data search engines expect.

Migrate

Platform to platform, host to host, WordPress to static. We keep the URL map intact, redirect what moves, and check the index afterwards so traffic does not fall off a cliff.

Speed up

Image and font weight, render-blocking scripts, layout shift, caching. Measured before and after on the real URLs, not on a lab score.

Fix

Broken checkout, missing pages, a template that repeats the same title across hundreds of products, a contact form nobody receives. Small, defined, priced.

Why plain HTML and PHP by default

A site that renders as static files is fast, cheap to host, hard to break, and easy to hand over. When a platform earns its subscription (order management, payments, a team that needs a dashboard), we will say so, and we will use it. What we avoid is a site whose uptime depends on a plan someone forgets to renew.
